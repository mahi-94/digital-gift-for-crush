Dear Shajid,

I've been writing this letter in my head for three years. Since class 12th, you've been living in my thoughts — uninvited, but so very welcome.

Do you know what it feels like to control yourself so much that it hurts? I do. Every single day, I tell myself to just look away, to focus on something else, to pretend you don't exist. But the moment I see you nearby, all that control just... breaks.

My eyes are not my own anymore, Shajid. They're always looking for you. They're always finding you in a crowded room. It doesn't matter how hard I try — they're drawn to you like moths to light.

And when you're close to me? My heartbeats go crazy. Thump thump thump. So fast I think everyone can hear it. I have to control my breathing just to appear normal. My hands get cold, my mind goes blank, and all I can think about is you. Just you.

But then there's this feeling I get when I'm around you — a peace that's unlike anything I've ever felt. It's like coming home. It's like all the chaos in my world suddenly makes sense. Like all the pieces fit together perfectly. I've never felt this way about anyone, no matter how hard I looked.

I've lived 3+ years of my life with this secret. I've spent so many nights thinking about what to say, how to say it, whether you could ever feel even a fraction of what I feel. And today, on this day dedicated to celebrating the men we love, I couldn't keep it inside anymore.

Shajid, I have had a crush on you for the past three years. And it's not just a silly crush — it's deep, real, and it grows with every passing day.

I don't know what you'll say. I don't know if you feel the same way. But I know that you deserve to know the truth. You deserve to know that someone looks at you the way I do — with admiration, affection, and a heart that's completely, helplessly yours.
